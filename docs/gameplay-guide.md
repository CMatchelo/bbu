# BBU – Gameplay Guide

Welcome to **BBU**, a university basketball management sim where you step into the shoes of a head coach at a Brazilian university. Your goal is to build a winning program, develop your players, improve your facilities, recruit the next generation of talent, and ultimately compete for a national championship.

---

## Getting Started

When you launch the game, you'll choose between **New Game** or **Load Game**.

In a **New Game**, you'll:
1. **Pick a university** from a pool of 50 Brazilian universities, each split across 5 regional leagues.
2. **Choose a difficulty** — Easy, Medium, or Hard — which affects how tough your opponents are and how the season plays out.
3. **Sign your first roster** — at the start of Week 1, you'll select the players who will join your program for the season.

---

## The Season at a Glance

A full season runs for **36 weeks**. During those weeks, your team plays matches, your players grow, and you manage every aspect of the program behind the scenes. After Week 36, the best teams in the country enter the **National Playoffs**.

Here's the rhythm of a typical week:
- Your team plays a match (you only play once per week, though the CPU teams play their own games).
- Before the match, you set your lineup and game plan.
- After the match, player skills update based on their performance and your training setup.
- In between matches, you manage practice, recruiting, facilities, and injured players.

---

## Your Coaching Dashboard

The **Coach Dashboard** is your home base. From here you can see:
- A summary of your last and upcoming match.
- Your **stat leaders** — which players are putting up the best numbers.
- Players who are **unavailable** due to injury or academic suspension.
- The **league standings** or **playoff bracket**, depending on where you are in the season.

Use the side menu to navigate to every section of the game.

---

## Managing Your Roster

### The Team Page

The **Team** page shows all your players and has three tabs:

- **Skills** — Displays each player's 10 individual skills: Layup, 2-Point Shooting, 3-Point Shooting, Passing, Ball Handling, Speed, Rebounding, Defense, Steal, and Block. Each skill is rated from 1 to 99.
- **Stats** — Shows each player's season statistics: points, assists, rebounds, field goals, 3-pointers, turnovers, blocks, and steals.
- **Education** — Lets you assign tutors to players whose grades are slipping. This matters — if a player's grade falls below 70, they become **academically ineligible** and cannot play until their grades recover.

### Player Attributes

Beyond skills, each player has hidden attributes that shape how they develop and perform:
- **Potential** — Their ceiling. High-potential players will improve faster and reach higher skill levels over time.
- **Stamina** — Affects how well they hold up during a game. Tired players perform worse and are more likely to get injured.
- **Intelligence** — Influences how well they execute plays.
- **Injury Proneness** — Some players are more fragile than others.

---

## Developing Your Players

### Individual Practice

On the **Player Development** page, you assign each player one specific skill to focus on for the week. That skill will develop faster than the rest based on their performance in matches.

All players improve a little each game just from playing. But the skill you assign for practice will grow significantly faster — so be strategic about what you prioritize for each player.

A player's **match performance** also matters: if a player had a great game using a particular skill, they'll develop that skill more from it.

> **Note:** If a player's grades are below 70, they won't develop **at all** that week, even if they're practicing.

### Team Practice (Play Systems)

On the **Team Practice** page, you distribute **10 practice points** across your team's offensive and defensive play systems.

**Offensive Plays:**
- Pick & Roll
- Motion
- Isolation
- 5-Out
- Fast Break
- Post-Up

**Defensive Plays:**
- Man-to-Man
- Zone
- Perimeter Pressure
- Full Court Press
- Switch Everything
- Packed Paint

The more points you put into a play, the more **familiar** your team becomes with it over time. Familiarity matters during matches — your team runs plays they know better more effectively.

## Playing a Match

### Before the Game: Team Selection

Before each match, you go through the **Team Selection** screen where you:
- **Pick your 5 starters** (one per position: PG, SG, SF, PF, C).
- **Order your offensive and defensive plays** by priority. The plays higher on the list are more likely to be called during the game.

### During the Game

Matches are simulated possession by possession with a live play log. You'll see each possession unfold in real time: who shot, who assisted, who rebounded.

You can adjust the simulation speed — slow, normal, or fast — or let it run on its own.

Each team has **6 timeouts** per game. During a timeout, you can:
- **Substitute players** — swap out tired or struggling players.
- **Adjust your play order** — change your offensive and defensive priorities on the fly.

Matches are divided into **4 quarters**. Keep an eye on player stamina — players who spend a lot of time on the court will get tired, and tired players are more likely to make mistakes or get injured.

You can **save and exit** to the main menu at any time during a match and come back to it later.

---

## Keeping Players Healthy

The **Medical Department** page shows you all injured players on your roster, including what kind of injury they have and how long they're expected to be out.

Injuries happen randomly during games. A player who is fatigued or has high injury proneness is more at risk. Once injured, a player cannot play — but they can still practice and develop.

The level of your **Medical Center** facility affects how quickly injured players recover. A better facility means faster returns.

---

## Scouting & Recruiting

This is how you build for the future. High school players are generated each season, and it's your job to find the best ones and bring them to your program.

### Scouting

On the **Scouting Board**, you can scout up to **2 high school players at the same time**. The more you scout a player, the more information you'll uncover about their skills and potential.

You can also hire up to **2 tutors** for your scouted players. A tutored recruit:
- Won't see their grades drop.
- Has a better chance of passing the SAT (a requirement to join your program).

Before you can send a player a **Letter of Intent**, you need to have scouted them enough to meet a minimum threshold.

### Recruiting Board

The **Recruiting Board** is a table of all the high school players you've scouted so far. From here, you can:
- See their information and how interested they are in your university.
- Send a **Letter of Intent** to players you want to recruit.

> A player who has already committed to another university cannot be scouted or offered a Letter of Intent.

### Commitments

The **Commitments** page shows all the high school players who have accepted your Letter of Intent. They will officially join your roster at the start of the next season.

Your **Academic Prestige** facility level increases the chances that a recruit will accept your offer.

## Building Your University

On the **University** page, you can request improvements to your facilities. There are **6 facility types**, each upgradeable from Level 1 to Level 5:

| Facility | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Court** | Improves player development in Layup, 2-Point Shooting, 3-Point Shooting, Passing, and Ball Handling. |
| **Gym** | Improves player development in Speed, Rebounding, Steals, Defense, and Blocking. |
| **Medical Center** | Helps injured players recover faster. |
| **Physio Department** | Reduces how much stamina players lose while they're on court, and speeds up how fast they recover while sitting on the bench. |
| **Education Support** | Helps keep player grades up, reducing academic suspensions. |
| **Academic Prestige** | Increases the chances of recruits accepting your Letter of Intent. |

### How Facility Improvements Work

Improving a facility is not guaranteed. You submit a request and the **University Administration** decides whether to approve it.

- You need a **reputation of at least 60** for any improvement to be considered.
- The higher your reputation above 60, the better the chances of approval.
- If approved, your reputation **drops to 40** as a cost of the process, so plan your requests carefully.

---

## Reputation

Your **reputation** reflects how well you're doing as a coach. It goes up when you win (especially by large margins) and goes down when you lose.

Reputation matters because:
- It determines whether the university will approve your **facility improvement requests**.
- It represents your standing in the program.

Win consistently to build it up — but remember that requesting a facility improvement will cost you, so don't request one right before a tough stretch of games.

---

## The League & Standings

**50 universities** are split into **5 regional leagues** of 10 teams each. Every team plays through the 36-week regular season, and standings are determined by win-loss record.

On the **Leagues** page, you can see the full standings for your regional league and view all match results.

On the **Stats** page, you can browse statistics for every player across the entire country — useful for tracking where your players rank nationally.

---

## The Playoffs

After Week 36, the **32 best teams** in the country qualify for the **National Playoffs**:
- Each regional league sends its **top 6 finishers**.
- The **two best 7th-place teams** across all leagues also qualify.

Seeding is based on overall record: the top seed faces the lowest, and so on.

### Playoff Format

| Round | Format |
|---|---|
| Round 1 (32 → 16) | Best of 3 |
| Round 2 (16 → 8) | Best of 5 |
| Quarterfinals (8 → 4) | Best of 7 |
| Semifinals (4 → 2) | Best of 7 |
| Finals (2 → 1) | Best of 7 |

Win your series to advance. The last team standing is crowned **National Champion**.

The **Champions** page keeps a historical record of every national champion from past seasons.

---

## End of Season

Once the playoffs are over, the game wraps up the season:
- You'll see the **National Champion** and the **5 Regional Champions**.
- **Stat leaders** are highlighted across points, assists, rebounds, 3-pointers, and steals.
- **Committed recruits join your roster** for the next season.
- **Players in their final year graduate** and leave the program.
- All returning players **age one year** and carry their skills and stats into the next season.
- A **new pool of high school recruits** is generated for you to scout.

Then the cycle begins again — a new season, new challenges, and new opportunities to build your dynasty.

---

## Quick Tips

- **Watch your grades.** A player with grades below 70 can't play and won't develop — assign tutors early.
- **Don't neglect stamina.** Rotate your lineup during matches to keep players fresh, especially your key players.
- **Build reputation before requesting facilities.** Requesting too early means a higher chance of rejection and wasted reputation.
- **Scout early and often.** You can only scout 2 players at once, so start early in the season to cover more ground.
- **Match your play order to your roster.** If your best players are big men, lean on Post-Up and Pick & Roll. If you have shooters, prioritize 5-Out and Motion.
- **Practice points compound over time.** A play you consistently invest in becomes a weapon. Spreading points too thin means no play ever becomes truly effective.
